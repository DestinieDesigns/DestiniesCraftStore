DESTINIE'S CRAFT STORE — POSTING KIT
====================================

WHAT'S IN THIS ZIP
- index.html — your whole store as ONE file. Every photo is built into it,
  so this single file is all a hosting site needs.
- photos/ — your original product photos (3 per item), kept by item.
  Handy as backups, or for posting items on Facebook or anywhere else.
- destinie-store-photo-prompt.md — the master prompt that keeps product
  photos consistent if another AI makes your photo sets.

HOW TO POST THE STORE (easiest: Netlify Drop)
1. Unzip this file.
2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the unzipped folder (the one containing index.html) onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a live web address for the store. Done.

(GitHub Pages also works: create a repository, upload index.html, and turn
on Pages in the repo settings.)

ONE THING TO KNOW
This zip holds the shopper version of the store — the door, walk, displays,
and claim pages exactly as designed. The OWNER SIGN-IN version lives inside
Muse: it needs its saved memory to hold your password and your edits, and
that can't be zipped into a file. So:
- Keep making your owner edits in the Muse app version — that's the master.
- When you want the posted copy refreshed to match, the export can be
  re-made from the current store in a couple of minutes.
